# Artist avatar credits

MedNexus includes 24 fixed SVG portraits assembled from artist-designed illustration components. No generative AI was used for these portraits. The assets are served locally from public/avatars/artists.

## Open Peeps
12 portraits: Amani, Kofi, Ama, Nia, Kojo, Zuri, Esi, Kwame, Abena, Sena, Nana and Adwoa.
Artist: Pablo Stanley.
Original collection: https://www.openpeeps.com/
License: CC0 1.0 https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Adaptation: DiceBear Open Peeps illustration parts, with fixed hairstyles, expressions, skin tones, glasses and background colors curated for MedNexus.

## Adventurer
12 portraits: Nova, Atlas, Sage, Ember, River, Orion, Luna, Theo, Iris, Finn, Cleo and Kai.
Artist: Lisa Wischofsky.
Original collection and attribution: https://www.dicebear.com/styles/adventurer/
License: CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Adaptation: DiceBear Adventurer illustration parts, with fixed hairstyles, skin tones, glasses, freckles and background colors curated for MedNexus. These are adaptations, not an endorsement by the artist.

Each asset retains DiceBear's embedded creator/license metadata. Credits are also linked in store item details.
The build-time illustration packages are not required by the app at runtime.
